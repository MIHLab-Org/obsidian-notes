## 🎯 Course Overview

**Course title and number:** Introduction to Philosophy

**Semester and year:** Summer 2026

**Meeting days and times:**

**Central question this course addresses: **
    **Other Worldly Redemption: Virtual Love and Real Sanctification**
	- Gabriel Marcel has argued that science and technology reduce experiences of wonder.
	- Descartes and Denett argue that this is the proposed intention of science and technology
	- Helen De Cruz argues that science and technology open up new avenues of wonder
	- Following Eleonore Stump, I argue that what she is describing is not new science and technology, but old story telling
	- I then describe story telling as an old technology that creates new avenues of meaning and wonder, which Stump calls “other worldly redemption”.
