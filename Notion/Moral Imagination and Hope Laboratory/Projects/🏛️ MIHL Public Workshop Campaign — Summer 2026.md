## 💼 LinkedIn Campaign

**Why LinkedIn works here:** Your Stetson affiliation, PhD, and Coast Guard background are visible before anyone reads a word. That context changes everything. LinkedIn is professional, credibility-first, and the right place to reach educators, veterans program coordinators, and philosophy-adjacent professionals.

**Two uses of LinkedIn:**

1. **Direct outreach** — find specific contacts (library programmers, VSO coordinators, philosophy dept chairs) and send a connection request + follow-up note. Higher conversion than cold email.
2. **Long-form posts** — substantive professional content that builds credibility over time and gets shared.

**The framing rule:** Lead with curiosity, never evaluation. Questions work. Judgments don't.
