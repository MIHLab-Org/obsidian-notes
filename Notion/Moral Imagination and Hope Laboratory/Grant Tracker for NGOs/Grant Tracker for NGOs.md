## About the template

This Grant Tracker template is designed to help organizations efficiently manage and monitor their grants from application to final reporting. It provides a centralized space to track deadlines, budgets, deliverables, and compliance requirements, ensuring transparency and accountability throughout the grant lifecycle.

<!-- Column 1 -->
> [!note] 📌
> **How to Use the Template:**
> - In the Grant Tracker, you can view main information about grants, including deadlines and amounts requested or received, etc.
> - Each grant has its own page (see [[NEH-Collaborative Research]] ). To add a new grant, simply click the blue “New” button located at the top right of the table and add the relevant information.
> - The Grant Tracker offers several views, allowing you to see upcoming deadlines, projects currently in drafting, and more.
> - You can customize everything in this template as you see fit.
