# Integrated 8-Week Curriculum

## “Building Philosophical VR: From Concept to Published Experience”

### Complete Path: Pedagogy + Unity Essentials + AI Personas + Launch

---

## Overview

This curriculum integrates:
- **Philosophical pedagogy** (our 6 modules)
- **Unity Essentials** (official Unity learning path, 10:35)
- **Persona system** (hierarchical AI routing)
- **Real app building** (Experience Machine MVP)

**Result**: Students progress from zero VR knowledge → published game with AI-driven Socratic guides on Meta Quest Store.

**Total student time: 120-140 hours** (10-14 hours/week)
- 50+ hours: Philosophical content & design
- 35+ hours: Unity Essentials (embedded)
- 20+ hours: Persona integration & routing
- 15+ hours: Building Experience Machine
- 5-10 hours: Launch & marketing
