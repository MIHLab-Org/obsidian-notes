E.g.,  is difficult to show, but if we suppose *P*, then we can “obtain a perfectly good explanation of *X*”. Claim: The earth floats on water (like a log)

- Assume *P*, the earth floats on water
	- If *P → Q*
- Therefore *Q*
- As such, *P*, the earth floats on water, is true.
