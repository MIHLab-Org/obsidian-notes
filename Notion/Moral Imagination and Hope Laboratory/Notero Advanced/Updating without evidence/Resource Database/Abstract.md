Sometimes we are unreliable at fulfilling our doxastic plans.
    we plan to be fully confident in all truths
    however, it is likely that we will be confident in some falsehoods by mistake
    in some of these cases [where we become confident in falsehoods by mistake], there is information acting as evidence
        our beliefs are perfectly discriminating with respect to any non-trivial facts
        and there is standard expected-accuracy-based justification for [planning to conditionalize on this evidence]
        this planning-oriented justification extends to some cases where we do not have transparent evidence
            e.g., our beliefs are not perfectly discriminating with respect to any non-trivial facts
            in other cases, accuracy considerations do not tell us to plan to conditionalize on any information at all
                rather they plan to tell us to follow a different updating rule
            in these cases, we can act without evidence
        we can plan to act even when we do not have transparent evidence
    so in the absence of evidence, accuracy considerations can guide our doxastic plan

Even then, we can develop a doxastic plan
