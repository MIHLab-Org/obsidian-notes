Minded vs. non-minded beings?
	- ability to read [sight, read] 
	- agents, can act with intention; efficient action

Capacities

- Two kinds of minded beings
	- perception → action
	- perception → thinking → action
		- Weigh up competing sources of evidence
		- consider consequences of a hypothetical action
		- adopt the perspective of other creatures
		- manipulate the world in order to see what would happen under certain conditions
		- Thought also enables us to exploit our capacities for action in new ways expanding the kind of control that we have over our environment

Mental
    Faculties
	- Memory, perception of the past
	- Emotion, with its strange and strong connection to perceiving, thinking, and acting
	- Imagination, enables us to envisage how things may turn out in the future, or hypothetical occurrences, how things might have been

Those are faculties, but what is occurring inside us?
    Events: They are particular happenings
		- bodily sensations
			- headaches
			- itches
			- pains
			- thirst
			- hunger
		- Mental Sensations
			- drowsiness
			- lightheadness
		- Cognitive
			- Understanding a joke
			- Or being insulted
    Processes, transitions between events
		- Practical reasoning: deciding where to go for dinner
		- Theoretical reasoning
		- visualization
    States
		- Standing States
			- mental phenomena that do not ‘unfold over time’
				- beliefs
				-
