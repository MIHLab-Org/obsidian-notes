Claim: anarchists have claimed we would be better off without a state.
Response: Moral philosophy is concerned with what persons may and may not do to one another. A state is a collection of persons, and so moral philosophy is concerned with affairs of the state. Moral prohibitions established through via moral philosophy set the terms of legitimacy of a person’s, and therefore a state’s coercive power. Because this is a value claim, moral philosophy sets the background and boundary of political philosophy.
