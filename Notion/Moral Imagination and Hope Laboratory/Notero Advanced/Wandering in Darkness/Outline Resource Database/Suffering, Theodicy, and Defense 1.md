Summary: |2
    • The problem of evil requires understanding the nature of suffering.
    • We sometimes think that to suffer is to have our will (central desires of the heart) violated.
    • But this doesn’t fully capture the nature of suffering. To suffer is also to have our flourishing undermined.
    • Flourishing is tantamount to “being the best one can be”.
    • However, it is possible that we are not deeply committed to what would constitute our flourishing. Perhaps because we do not know or want would is necessary for our flourishing.
