We're reading david chalmers' reality+. 

This assignment will cover the 2nd part of the book on epistemology. 

I am assigning a dialogue based assignment where the students must form groups and each person will take on the personality of a philosopher from the reading. 

There is a primary question, Q: "can we know things?". 

They then have to look at different responses to this question. 

They then have to provide the best response to those responses. 

Q: "Can we know things?", A1: justified true belief, but skepticism, skepticism about senses, skepticism about evil demons, skepticism about being asleep, once those are all refuted, then simulation hypothesis we could be in a virtual world, e.g., brain in a vat, it should read like a conversation among a group of people, A2: cogito ergo sum, why does this fail? 

They should answer this, Q2: "can we prove there is an external world?"
