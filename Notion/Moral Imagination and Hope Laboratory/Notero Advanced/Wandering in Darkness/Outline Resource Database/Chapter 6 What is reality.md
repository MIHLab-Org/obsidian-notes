# What is Reality?

- Realism
	- Virtual Realism is the thesis that virtual reality is genuine reality
	- Simulation realism is the thesis that if we are in a simulation, then the objects around us are real
	- Virtual digitalism is the view that given virtual realism, digital objects are real
