“extraordinary intelligence” is unobservable

causal role
    Einstein has extraordinary intelligence $\rightarrow$ Einstein comes up with $e=mc^{2}$

> In psychology, there are many constructs that play this type of role in theories of human behavior; examples are constructs like extraversion, spatial ability, selfefficacy, and attitudes. Such variables are usually referred to as latent variables.

intelligence is a latent variable

We investigate the structure and effect of unobservables like intelligence through the analysis of interindividual differences [in] data 

by statistically relating covariation between
    observed variables to 
    latent variables

One way to do this is through the factor model.
    However, the fit of latent variable analysis does not prove the existence of causally operating latent variables, the fit of latent variable analysis models can be adduced as evidence supporting the hypothesis of the existence of the latent variable.

## The Argument

We will argue that, without a realist interpretation of latent variables, the use of latent variables analysis is hard to justify.
	1. latent variable typically appears in two distinct ways: as a formal–theoretical concept and as an operational– empirical concept.
	2. The formal and operational-empirical concepts need to be connected by a third ontological concept
	3. Three concepts are presented: 
		1. Realism
		2. Constructivisim
		3. Operationalism
	4. It is argued that a realism account of the latent variable is required to “maintain a consistent connection between the formal and empirical concept of the latent variable.
		4. The realist view requires an account of the relation between the latent variable and its indicators, for which causality is a natural candidate.
