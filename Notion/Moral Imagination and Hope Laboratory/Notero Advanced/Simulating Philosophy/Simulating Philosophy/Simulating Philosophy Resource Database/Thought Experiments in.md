Summary: |
  Thought experiments are hypothetical scenarios that challenge or substantiate a theory by serving as abstract models of more complex issues.

    1. Challenge theories by identifying a difficult case the theory cannot answer.
    2. Model situations that may be untestable using empirical methods.
    3. Less dangerous than real-world experiments.
