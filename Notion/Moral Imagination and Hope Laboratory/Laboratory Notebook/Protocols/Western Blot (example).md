## Materials

### Transfer step

**Roti-NC membrane**: 0.45 um pore size membrane

**CAPS buffer**: 10 mM CAPS in dH2O pH 11 (NaOH) 

**Transfer buffer**: CAPS buffer + 10% MeOH (1 L)

**XCell module**

Filter papers and sponges

use same cassette as the SDS-PAGE

### Blotting step

**TBS buffer 10x**: 24 g Tris + 88 g NaCl pH 7.6 (HCl) + 1 L H2O

**TBS-T**: 100 mL TBS 10x + 900 mL dH2O + 1 mL Tween20

**Blocking solution**: 3 % Milk or 3 % BSA in TBS-T (10 mL per each membrane)

**Primary antibody**: 50 uL Ab + 15 mL Blocking solution

**Secondary antibody: **5 uL Ab + 10 mL TBS-T

## Procedure

Perform an SDS-PAGE as usual, remember to use a **PreStained Ladder**.

### Transfer

1. Take a vessel and fill it with <u>cold</u> Transfer buffer. Soak there 4 blotting pads, 4 filter papers and the membrane.
2. In another vessel, soak the SDS-PAGE gel.
3. In the XCell module, place everything as depicted in the picture. Make sure to remove bubbles.
4. Filled up everything with Transfer buffer.
5. Close the chamber and run at 30 V for 75 min (remember that small proteins are transferred faster than big ones).

![[Untitled 8.png]]

### Blotting

6. Place membrane in a vessel and add the blocking solution (usually 10 mL per membrane is enough).
7. Incubate for at least 1.30 h
8. Add primary antibody (usually 10 mL are enough) for 2 h at RT or O/N at 4°C
9. Wash the membranes 3 times with TBS-T
10. Add secondary antibody for at least 1 h at RT
11. Wash 4 the membrane 4 times with TBS-T
12. Detect bands - ask to the chemistry building

### Good to know

Prepare new buffer after 5 transfers.
