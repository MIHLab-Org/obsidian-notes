## Take home Messages

- The ubiquity of virtual tools for navigating our environments are examples of our mind’s extension into our devices.
- Therefore humans and virtual systems are tightly coupled.
- As such: 
	- Virtuality gives us new ways to think about our spaces.
	- Can reconfigure how we think of morality and the self.
	- Increase our capacities, but it is up to us how they do so.
