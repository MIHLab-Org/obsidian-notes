## 🏗️ Suggested Structure

- *Introduction** — State your thesis clearly. Describe the steps of your argument concisely (not as an outline, but as a narrative)
- *Body**
- Premise 1 — state clearly, provide sub-argument if controversial
- Premise 2 — state clearly, provide sub-argument if controversial
- Counter-argument — state the strongest objection and respond to it
- *Conclusion** — Tie up loose ends. Consider implications or further questions
