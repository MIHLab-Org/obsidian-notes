# MIHLab Great Books in Space: Program Research and Planning Guide

**The program:** participants read philosophical texts, identify a thought experiment the reading raises, and then role-play that thought experiment inside a space-simulation game such as Elite Dangerous, EVE Online or Star Citizen.

**Short answer:** Great Books discussion programs and philosophy-through-games courses both exist, but nothing found combines them into a read, design and role-play cycle in a shared space sim. Two partners stand out: the **Great Books Foundation** for the discussion method, and the **BGG (Bruh Got Game) Foundation** in St. Louis for youth gaming programs.

## 2. BGG (Bruh Got Game) Foundation

- **Founder:** Robert L. (Rob) Powell III, who previously worked in management at Sony PlayStation and grew up in the St. Louis Public Schools system.
- **Mission:** use gaming and esports to empower youth, build leadership and open STEM career pathways, especially for socioeconomically disadvantaged young people, leading them toward higher education.
- **Programs:** youth tournaments (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, Brawlhalla, 2XKO), Gaming at the Library with the St. Louis County Library, mentoring for esports scholarships, and a gaming room at AnimeStL.
- **Location:** St. Louis, MO 63146.
- **Why it matters:** a proven model for running youth gaming programs with libraries, which could be a template for hosting Great Books in Space sessions.

## 5. Insurance and compliance

- **Online-only sessions:** general liability, errors and omissions, and cyber coverage, as in the games studio report. TechInsurance averages are about $30, $83 and $150 a month.
- **In-person sessions with minors:** youth esports insurers such as Players Health and eSportsInsurance offer participant and spectator liability. Florida doesn't mandate it, but going without means unlimited personal liability.
- **Background checks:** a national sex-offender registry check is the minimum; a comprehensive criminal check is the gold standard, renewed every two years.
- **Waivers:** waivers signed for minors may not hold up, depending on state law.
- **Online safety:** commercial games have open chat and other players. Use private groups or sessions, check each game's age rating and terms (many require players to be 13+ or 18+), and get parental consent.

Links: [Players Health Florida](https://insurance.playershealth.com/esports-insurance-florida) · [eSportsInsurance](https://esportsinsurance.com/quote/gaming/) · [Youth sports insurance guide](https://archive.sports-scoops.com/news/youth-amateur-sports-insurance-complete-guide-2026)

## 7. Session design (proposal)

1. **Read:** a short philosophical text, discussed with Shared Inquiry.
2. **Find the thought experiment:** for example, Parfit on personal identity, Nozick's experience machine, or Descartes' evil demon.
3. **Design:** map it onto game mechanics, such as EVE's clones for identity or Star Citizen's death-of-a-spaceman narrative for meaningful mortality.
4. **Play:** role-play the scenario together in a private group.
5. **Debrief:** return to the text and ask whether the play changed anyone's intuitions.

## 10. Marketing strategy

**Core message:** read great philosophy, then live its thought experiments inside a space game. No program found combines the two (Section 1), which is the selling point.

### 10.1 How comparable programs reach people

| Program | How it reaches participants | Lesson for MIHLab |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Great Books Foundation | Trains educators and schools to run Shared Inquiry; online Virtual Academy draws students across North America | Recruit through teachers and homeschool networks; offer an online cohort |
| Great Books Summer Program | Sells to parents as college preparation and "learning to think on your feet" | Lead with the skills parents value |
| BGG Foundation | Free tournaments and "Gaming at the Library" events with the St. Louis County Library; local news and radio coverage | Partner with libraries for free or low-cost sessions; pitch local media |
| CSU Chico course | Campus news story on a philosophy-and-games class | A Stetson news story could launch the program |
| Jim's YouTube work | Philosophy-through-space-games videos | Use clips as free samples of a session |

### 10.3 Pricing messages (proposal)

- **Library sessions:** free or sponsored, to build the audience.
- **Online season:** a per-person or per-family fee covering readings and sessions; price after costs are known (Section 8).
- **EVE Online is free to start,** which lowers the barrier for families.
