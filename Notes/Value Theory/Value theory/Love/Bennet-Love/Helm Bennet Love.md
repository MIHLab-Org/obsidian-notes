## Love as Union

The union view claims that love consists in the formation of (or the desire to form) some significant kind of union, a “we.”

- Aristotle (cf. Sherman 1993)
- Montaigne ([E]) 
- Hegel (1997); 
- contemporary proponents include 
	- Solomon (1981, 1988), 
	- Scruton (1986), 
	- Nozick (1989), 
	- Fisher (1990), 
	- Delaney (1996).

- Formation of Union
- Desire for Formation of Union
