## The Truth Rule and the Nap

> My daughter is at the age where she’s dropping her nap. She takes an afternoon nap roughly half the time. On the days when she has a good nap, she is usually in a good mood in the evening. On the days when she doesn’t have a good nap, she is often really cranky. What I am wondering is what degree of belief I should have that she will be cranky this evening, _after_ I find out whether [or not] she naps.

Truth rule: plan to be _sure_ that she will be cranky if she really will be cranky, or else be _sure_ she won’t be cranky if she really won’t be.

* if daughter naps, then in Nap-and-not-Cranky state, she will not be cranky, so place all credence in that state
* if no nap, then no-Nap-and-not-Cranky state , she will be cranky, and place all credence in this state
* so either in nap or no-nap state
* truth rule would be inaccurate
