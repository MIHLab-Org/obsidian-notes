## Introduction

Can an open LLM-powered agent, which struggles with basic tasks, improve through collaboration with others?

Yes: By structuring communication across turns, MINDFORGE also contributes to multi-turn LLM agent research [21, 22], showing that open models can match GPT-4 through collaboration alone.

Why: 

(1) perspective-taking through a causal ToM template [23] linking agent percepts, beliefs, desires, and actions; [Kanishk Gandhi, Jan-Philipp Fränken, Tobias Gerstenberg, and Noah Goodman. Understanding  social reasoning in language models with language models. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems, 36, 2024.]
(2) a natural language communication interface between agents; and (3) memory updates following the Soar cognitive architecture [24: John E Laird. The Soar cognitive architecture. MIT press, 2019.]
