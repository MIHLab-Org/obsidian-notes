## Abstract

The externalist says that your evidence could fail to tell you what evidence you do or not do have.

If externalism is correct, then the orthodox Bayesian learning norms of conditionalization and reflection are inconsistent with each other.

The advice of conditionalization will be followed by anyone whose learning dispositions maximize expected accuracy.

Therefore, an externalist Bayesian should reject conditionalization.

## Introduction

Orthodox Bayesian Epistemology: Models rational uncertainty. 

1. OBE internalists take it for granted that your evidence will always tell you what your total evidence is. However, OBE struggles to model uncertainty about what evidence you do or do not possess.
2. OBE externalists think that your evidence could fail to tell you what evidence you have or don’t have.
3. If the externalists are correct, then what? Well then the orthodox Bayesian learning norms of conditionalization and reflection are inconsistent with each other.
	1. The externalists should reject the Orthodox learning norm (updating rule) of conditionalization (Salow 2018).
		1. If an externalist Bayesian follows conditionalization, then they will be capable of engaging in acts of deliberate self-delusion. Repeated ‘learning’ from experience is one way as to raise their rational credence in some (even false) proposition as high as they like. Assuming evidentialism—that is, assuming that the rationality of your doxastic states is determined by the evidence you possess—externalism entails that your evidence could fail to tell you whether your doxastic states are rational or not. As such then, epistemic akrasia begins to look rational. Given it, one might believe both that it will rain and that it’s irrational to believe that it will rain.
		2. Therefore, advice offered by the Externalist* will be followed by anyone whose learning dispositions maximize expected accuracy and anyone who follow this theory’s advice will be incapable of engaging in deliberate self-delusion.
	2. Therefore externalists should instead adopt updating rule*.
