What kinds of interactions can we have with the newest artificial systems based on generative AI technology, 

We suggest that a taxonomy of affordances can be used to develop a nuanced understanding of how to describe our interactions with them. 

Elaborating on a spectrum of affordances, ranging from mere environmental instances to quasi-social up to full-fledged forms of social affordances, we will come to the conclusion that with current AI systems, we can do things together (act jointly), but so far, we cannot live with them.
