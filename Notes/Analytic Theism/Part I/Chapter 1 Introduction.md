## The nature and scope of philosophy

The nature and scope of philosophy is an attempt to solve questions that remain elusive to the investigative techniques used in other disciplines, i.e., the hard sciences and literature.

As such, it brings together two areas of philosophical inquiry: contemporary 'analytic' metaphysics and 'analytic' philosophy of religion.

## The nature and scope of metaphysics

### The nature of metaphysics

Nature and structure of reality, fundamental nature and structure of reality as a whole.

Natural sciences only focus on the structure of physical reality, or nature and workings of biological organisms. 

I.e., the nature of existence, objects and their properties, space and time, modality, causality, etc.

#### Positivist Era

Considered traditional metaphysics to be speculative and unverifiable: see Rudolf Carnap, Hans Hahn, and Otto Neurath. The only real things where those things experienced through sense impressions and objects directly observable (or verifiable by direct observation).
