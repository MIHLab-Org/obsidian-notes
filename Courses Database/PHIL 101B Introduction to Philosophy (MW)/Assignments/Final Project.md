### 4. Final Project (80 points)

**Due**: Last day of class (12/11/2026)

**Format**: Individual written project (~2500-3000 words)

**Structure**: Three-philosopher exploration

**Your task:**

1. **Choose ONE philosophical topic** (e.g., "Is consciousness physical?" or "Can knowledge be objective?" or "Do moral truths exist?")
2. **Select THREE philosophers** you've encountered in the course
3. **For each philosopher**, structure your analysis as:
	- **Philosopher's Position**: What does this philosopher argue about your topic?
	- **Your Initial Intuition** (before reading): What did you think before encountering this view?
	- **Game Experience**: Describe a game scenario (from class or one you design) that explored this philosopher's position
	- **Your Intuition After**: How did engaging with the game-based thought experiment affect your thinking?
	- **Why?**: Explain what about the game experience changed (or confirmed) your intuition
4. **Synthesis Section** (final 500-750 words):
	- Summary of each philosopher's position
	- Which philosopher's view do you find most convincing? Why?
	- Which did you initially resist? Why?
	- Your final position on the topic and how you arrived at it
	- Reflection on the role of thought experiments in your philosophical development

**Grading Rubric (80 points):**

- Philosophical accuracy & depth (20 pts)
- Quality of game-experience reflection (20 pts)
- Clarity of intuition tracking across philosophers (20 pts)
- Synthesis & original thinking (15 pts)
- Writing quality & organization (5 pts)
