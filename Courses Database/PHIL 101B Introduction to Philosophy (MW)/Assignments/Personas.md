### 3. Philosopher Personas (75 points total)

**Frequency**: 3 persona creations (once per game session cycle)

**Format**: Group project (3 students)

**Duration**: 2-week development cycle for each persona

**What you do:**

1. Draw a philosopher's name from a hat (not a philosopher you're reading weekly)
2. Spend 2 weeks researching that philosopher's position on a specific philosophical problem
3. Create a **Claude AI persona** that embodies that philosopher
4. Write the system prompt that instructs Claude to ask Socratic questions (not give answers) in the style of that philosopher
5. **Test your persona**: Have peers interact with it and provide feedback
6. Refine based on testing
7. Submit: System prompt + documentation + testing feedback

**Example**: You draw "Immanuel Kant." Your task is to create a Kant persona that, when asked "Is morality objective?", responds with Socratic questions like:

- "Tell me, what do you mean by 'objective'? Does it require universal agreement?"
- "Hmm, but couldn't something be universally obligatory without depending on our desires?"
- "Have you considered the distinction between hypothetical and categorical imperatives?"

**Grading Rubric (25 points each):**

- Philosophical accuracy of persona (8 pts)
- Quality of Socratic questioning (8 pts)
- Clarity of system prompt documentation (5 pts)
- Evidence of testing & iteration (4 pts)
