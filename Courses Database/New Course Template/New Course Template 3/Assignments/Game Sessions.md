### 2. Game Sessions (45 points total)

**Frequency**: 3 sessions throughout semester (assigned dates on calendar)

**Format**: Group participation (3 students per group) + individual reflection

**Duration**: 45-60 min gameplay + written reflection

**What happens:**

- Your group plays through a philosopher-guided game experience in the Experience Machine
- The experience tests your intuitions on a philosophical problem
- You reflect individually on your experience

**Deliverable**: Short reflection (250-400 words) including:

- Topic of the philosophical problem
- Which philosopher/persona guided the experience
- Your initial intuition (before)
- The game event/scenario
- Your intuition after
- Why did (or didn't) the experience change your thinking?

**Grading Rubric (15 points each):**

- Engagement & reflection clarity (5 pts)
- Philosophical insight (5 pts)
- Connection between game experience & intuition change (5 pts)
