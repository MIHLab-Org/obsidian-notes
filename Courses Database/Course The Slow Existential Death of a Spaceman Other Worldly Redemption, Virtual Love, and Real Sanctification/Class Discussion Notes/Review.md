# Virtual Worlds

## Chapter 1

### Question: Is this the real life?

Traditional Versions of the Question:
	- Zhuangzi’s butterfly dream
	- Narada’s Transformation
	- Plato’s Cave
    Three Contemporary Versions of the Questions
		- Did Zhuangzi dream he was a butterfly, or is he a butterfly dreaming he is Zhuangzi?
		- For Narada, what is real, what is illusory?
		- 


Look around you. Is this the real life? You are probably looking at your bed. The wall. Clothes on the floor. Surely they all look real enough. 

You will eventually have to pick them up either way, won’t you? Like the prisoner in Plato’s cave, does it matter if you can  live a good life either way? 

But will you, or can you, if like Narada, it’s only an illusion? Is any ***experience*** ever an illusion? 

What about the dream you dreamt last night? How do you know you’re awake?  Zhuangzi leads us to ask, what makes you think that something is real, when you could be dreaming? 

What if you were still asleep? Would that be so bad?

Humans often use simulations to test out hypotheses as you probably well know. Now some of you are probably thinking that “sure, scientists test hypotheses, but I am an art major.” Artists also test out hypotheses. 

But unlike artists, philosophers call these possible worlds and thought experiments.
