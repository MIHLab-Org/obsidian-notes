**Self-Defense:**
    Killing and harming others are paradigmatic wrongs. And yet there is at least one intuitive exception to this prohibition—namely, killing or harming in self-defense, or in defense of others. With the exception of strict pacifists, there is broad consensus in morality and law that defensive harm can be permissible in cases like this. However, as we shall see, it is surprisingly difficult to explain the grounds and limits of this permission. Show to prominent attempts to explain the permissibility of self-defense. Then focus on the account that Thomson defends and why she defends it.
