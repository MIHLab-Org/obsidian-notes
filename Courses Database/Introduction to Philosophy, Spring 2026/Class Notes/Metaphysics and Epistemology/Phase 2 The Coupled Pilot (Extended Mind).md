> **Aim **of experiment.**
**To demonstrate how external symbols (HUD elements) drive cognitive processes in real-time, creating a "Coupled System" where the ship and pilot function as a single unit.

1. Objective: Locate and land at the provided planetary coordinates (Lat/Long).
2. Procedure: Do not look for visual landmarks. Instead, focus entirely on the HUD Coordinate Display (located above your fuel gauge) and your Heading Compass (the degrees at the top of your HUD).

# Materials

Elite Dangerous

Heading compass in HUD display

HUD Coordinate Display (above “fuel gage”, degrees at top of HUD)

# Method

The Precision Instrument Landing

3. Instead of a giant, unmistakable crater, the student is tasked with finding a station at a set of Planetary Coordinates (Latitude/Longitude).  The student must navigate to a specific point using the HUD's coordinate display and the heading compass.  Land at the station at the coordinates.
4. During this flight, the student isn't looking at the ground to "feel" their way down. They are performing Epistemic Actions:
	1. Coordinate Matching: They are altering the ship's pitch and yaw specifically to make the numbers on the HUD change. 
	2. They aren't "flying to a place"; they are "solving a numerical puzzle" that results in being at a place.
5. The Landing Hologram: Once they are over the spot and low enough, the holographic landing grid appears. This is another Epistemic Action that transforms complex 3D spatial alignment into a simpler task of aligning the blue circles.

### Reflection Questions

The Boundary Question: "When you were adjusting your heading to match the coordinates, were you 'thinking' about the math of a sphere, or were you simply reacting to the HUD? If you were reacting to the HUD, wasn't the ship's computer doing the 'navigational thinking' for you?"

The Parity Principle: "If a future neural implant gave you these coordinates directly in your 'mind's eye' (Case 3 in the Tetris example), would that feel any different from looking at the HUD? If the result and the process are the same, is there any reason to say the HUD isn't part of your mind?"

Epistemic vs. Pragmatic: "When you pitched the nose down to see the landing zone better, was that a Pragmatic Action (moving the ship to get closer) or an Epistemic Action (moving the ship to get better information)?"
