## 🎯 Big Questions This Week

- What could block the simulation from being real?
- What is virtual realism?
- How should we define reality?

---

## 🚧 Sim Blockers Revisited

| Sim Blocker | What it Claims |
| --- | --- |
| Sims can’t be conscious | Consciousness requires something non-simulable |
| Simulators avoid conscious sims | Ethical or practical reasons prevent creating aware sims |
| Sims won’t have minds like ours | Our kind of mind is a nonsim feature |
| Sims won’t experience large universes | The scale of our universe is a nonsim sign |

> Stepping back: The potential nonsim signs we’ve considered, such as consciousness and a large world, may decrease the probability that we’re in a simulation. At the same time, we need to weigh these against potential sim signs, such as the fact that we seem to be early in the universe, which may increase the probability.

---

## 🧠 Chalmers’s Virtual Realism

| Position | Claim |
| --- | --- |
| **Virtual Realism** | Virtual reality is genuine reality; virtual objects are real and not illusions |
| **Simulation Realism** | Objects in a simulation around us are real and not illusions |
| **Virtual Digitalism** | Objects in virtual reality are digital objects — structures of binary information |

---

## 🌍 Defining Reality

**Three senses of “reality”:**

1. **Everything that exists** — the entire cosmos
2. **A world or worlds** — physical and virtual spaces
3. **A property** — the property of being real

**Reality+:**

> The cosmos (everything that exists) contains many worlds (physical and virtual spaces), and the objects in those worlds are real.

## 📝 Instructor Notes

> [!note] 👨‍🏫
> This week consolidates the simulation argument and pivots to Chalmers’s positive response. The key move is that sim blockers don’t defeat the simulation hypothesis — they just set conditions. Chalmers’s virtual realism says: even if we are in a simulation, the things inside it are real. This is the thesis that drives the rest of the course and connects back to the opening question: does it matter whether this is the ‘real’ world?
