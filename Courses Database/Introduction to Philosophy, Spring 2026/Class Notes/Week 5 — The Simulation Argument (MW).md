## 🎯 Big Questions This Week

- What is happening in these chapters?
- What is Real? How do we know it is real? Why should we care?
- Are we probably sims?

---

## 🧠 The SimUniverse Thought Experiment

- From the inside, *SimUniverse* is indistinguishable from the universe it simulates
- Say the simulation contains 10 billion people
- If *SimUniverse* is a very popular program, millions or billions of devices may be running it
- Therefore: **there will be many more sims than non-sims**

---

## 📊 Bostrom’s Trilemma

At least one of the following must be true:

1. The human species is very likely to go **extinct** before reaching a posthuman stage
2. Any posthuman civilization is extremely unlikely to run **significant simulations** of their evolutionary history
3. We are almost certainly **living in a computer simulation**

Propositions 1 and 2 are **sim blockers** — they prevent the creation of many sims.

---

## 📝 The Simulation Argument Formalized

4. At least one in ten nonsim populations will each create a thousand sim populations
5. If (1), then at least 99% of intelligent beings are sims
6. If 99% of intelligent beings are sims, we are *probably* sims
7. ∴ We are *probably* sims

### Objections to Premise 1

- Intelligent sims are impossible
- Sims take too much computing power
- Nonsims will die out before creating sims
- Nonsims will choose not to create sims
- More nonsims than sims will be created

### Objections to Premise 3 — Are We Special?

> A sim sign is a feature that raises the probability that a creature is a sim — a feature that a sim is more likely to have than a nonsim.

**Potential sim signs** (features sims are *more* likely to have):

- We seem to be early in the universe

**Potential nonsim signs** (features sims are *less* likely to have):

- Consciousness
- Experience of a large universe

---

## 🔬 Bostrom’s Argument Restated

8. If there are no sim blockers, most humanlike beings are sims
9. If most humanlike beings are sims, we are probably sims
10. ∴ If there are no sim blockers, we are probably sims

### The Indifference Principle

- If there are many beings with the same sort of experience as me, I am equally likely to be any of them
- If 90% of beings with experiences like mine are sims, I should be 90% confident I am a sim

## 📝 Instructor Notes

> [!note] 👨‍🏫
> This week is the heart of the simulation argument. Students often find the indifference principle counterintuitive — it’s worth spending time on it. The key move is that we have no special reason to think we are nonsims rather than sims if sims vastly outnumber nonsims. Also introduce the metaphysics/epistemology/value theory framework: Chalmers isn’t just asking whether we’re sims but what would follow if we were. That question drives the rest of the course.
