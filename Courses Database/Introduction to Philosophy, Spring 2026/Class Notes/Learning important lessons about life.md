Logged in intending to join some tomato, group play in star citizen with gii. After receiving various promises to be added to the mission and not receiving an invite, I decided to stay and listen to the group’s conversations. As I listen, I think that I began to understand more that I probably wouldn’t be sent an invite. Typically any group play that I am invited to join, regardless of the group, are low risk/reward where I am offered a seat as a gunner on a large ship and these weren't the kind of engagements this group was participating in. 

I suspect that my kind of engagements, those that I can make anny meaningful contribution to, are oriented more towards lower level players like myself. I neither have the time to grind, nor the money to purchase in-game items that will make me useful in other ways. Therefore I typically will be just receiving a part of the payment without offering much in return. As a gunner on the other hand, I can make some somewhat meaningful contribution. 

On this particular occasion however, instead of logging out with conflicting feelings of identity and belonging, I stayed on while I attempted to grind in other single player missions to build my rep and pursue credits that will allow me to purchase other items that will make me a more valuable asset.

In these kinds of engagements, it might be helpful to find other players like myself, ones that I can show what I’ve learned and share missions with them. I wish I had thought of these possibilities sooner. 

As I listened however, I also began to understand just how long these players have played with one another. They’ve collectively gamed through significant changes in their lives, marriages, births, deaths, job changes. They’ve played together for years, the same game character, but not the same game character.
