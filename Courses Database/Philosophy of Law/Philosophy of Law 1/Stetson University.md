# Syllabus: Philosophy of Law

(Updated April 02, 2026)

### Course Description

We will examine theories which attempt to provide answers to such questions
as: What is a law? What makes a law valid or binding? Does one always
have a moral obligation to obey the law? What gives society the right to
punish people whose actions are harmful only to the individual, him/herself
(paternalism)? Does society have the right to harm those who break the law
(criminal punishment)? Should attempted crimes be punished less severely than
completed crimes? We will not spend much time discussing specific public policy
issues such as: Whether raising the drinking age saves lives, whether smoking
marijuana is a victimless crime (whether it harms others), or whether the death
penalty is a deterrent. We will not discuss what the law is; instead, we will
discuss what the law ought to be.
