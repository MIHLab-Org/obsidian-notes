> In a private case, litigants were charged court fees, and could settle the case without penalty. ## Athenian Law

In order to attempt such an action in bringing a suit against his father, Euthyprho must be very confident in his knowledge of right and wrong.

Suits were often brought on behalf of a perceived injustice committed against a family member, never on behalf of a stranger against a family member. As such, here, it seems Socrates is suggesting that there is a component of justice that extends beyond merely familial relationships. Further, a question here is how does Euthyphro have access to it.

## Arguments from Causation and Motion

1. Carrying and being carried
2. But a thing is not seen because it is visible, but visible because it is seen. (similar to a tree falling in the woods with noone there to hear it.)

### Argument about causation:

An object is in motion because of a previous event putting that object in motion.

Similarly, something is in a state of suffering because it suffered, namely something caused this state.

---

What is it to carry something? What is it to coerce someone? What is it to love another? First, in each of these cases, the act precedes the state. To carry something precedes the state wherein that thing is being carried. If someone is to be coerced, to be in the state of having been coerced, say into giving up your wallet to a would be robber, there must first be the act wherein the robber coerces the would be victim, say by producing a gun or doing something which would be construed as coercive. In similar fashion, if what is pious is merely that which is dear to the gods, well what is dear to the gods is merely what is loved by the gods. But the gods must love first, and that which is not loved by them because it is dear to them. So for something to be loved by the gods, pious, the gods would first have to love it.
