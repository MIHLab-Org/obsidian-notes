# Some examples:

## Possible Worlds

According to David Lewis's defense of modal realism, our world is but one of a plurality of worlds, and that the individuals that inhabit our world are only a few out of all the inhabitants of all the worlds. Lewis argues that the philosophical utility of modal realism is a good reason for believing that it is true. In everything, everywhere all at once, Michelle Yeoh (Evelyn Lang), discovers that the world she is familiar with is just one of many alternatives. Each includes an identity she herself could have assumed if things went a little differently than they had in this world. Compare the possible worlds account insinuated in EEAO with David Lewis's account. What is similar? What is different? What are the implications? Are they consistent with the events postulated in EEAO?
