[[Virtual realism]] I: Virtual reality is real—that is, the entities in virtual reality really exist.

Virtual realism II: virtual reality is genuine reality, with emphasis especially on the view that virtual objects are real and not an illusion.

[[Realism]] Something is real
	e.g., Moral realism = morality is real
		 Virtual realism = virtual objects are real
