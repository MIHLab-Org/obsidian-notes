## About this project

<!-- Column 1 -->
Professor Van Camp asks how should the contemporary dance community assess the authenticity of classical ballet reconstructions.  She highlights the work’s syntax, akin to its grammatical structure, assessing its authenticity of a dance by looking at its notation, music or dance scores, rehearsal notes, drawings, published criticisms etc. In this, I consider its semantics, or narrative meaning.
