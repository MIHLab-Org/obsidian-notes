Lab 2 — The Tutorial (Elite Dangerous)

Claim: Knowledge is justified true belief; or (Greco) true belief produced by ability.
Intuition: [Did you learn anything real from a simulated cockpit?]
Experiment: Complete the full Basic Flight tutorial set. Take three screenshots at distinct moments. Log every instrument you used and what it told you. Then answer: what do you now know, and is it knowledge-that, knowledge-how, or neither?
Finding: Does your answer survive Gettier? Is your flying success creditable to you in Greco's sense?

Lab 2b (optional) — "Do you know where I am?" (Star Citizen) — Instructor streams from New Babbage, microTech. Students answer: do you know where I am? Separate the answers that turn on knowledge from those that turn on reality.

Reconstruct the argument: Gettier's Smith/Jones case as premises and conclusion.
