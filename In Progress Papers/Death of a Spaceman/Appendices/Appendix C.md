### Appendix C. Grading Rubric (Thesis · Premises · Support · Counter-Arguments)

|Criterion|Excellent|Good|Needs Improvement|Unacceptable|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Thesis|A single, clear statement of the main point.|Thesis is present and fairly obvious, but not in one sharp sentence.|Thesis must be reconstructed from throughout the paper.|No real thesis.|
|Premises|Every reason clearly stated; sub-arguments provided for controversial claims; basic premises plausibly true.|Premises mostly clear; sub-arguments appear for controversial points.|Premises hard to identify; missing sub-arguments.|No real premises, or premises very likely false.|
|Support|Premises clearly and strongly support the thesis; argument is logically valid or makes the conclusion very plausible.|Premises support the thesis reasonably well.|Premises only weakly support the thesis; argument is invalid.|Premises do not support the thesis.|
|Counter-Arguments|Thoughtfully addresses both obvious and less-obvious objections with original replies.|Addresses the most obvious objections with reasonable responses.|Misses obvious objections, or responses are weak.|Ignores objections and opposing positions entirely.|
