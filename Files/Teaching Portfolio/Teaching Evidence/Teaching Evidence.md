#### Stetson University 2025-Present

I joined the faculty at Stetson University in Fall of 2025. I teach a 4/4 teaching load with between 15 and 28 students per course. I currently hold an above average teaching evaluation out of about 95 evaluations. In Fall 2025, I taught two 100 level logic courses, and a 300 level course in aesthetics, in Spring 2025 I taught two courses of Value, Meaning and Humanity's place in the world, one 100 level logic course and a 300 level course in the philosophy of law and one asymmetrical intro to philosophy course in Summer 2026. My summer course was unique in that all the assignments required the students to work in the space simulation open-world game Elite Dangerous and focused on questions of cognition, knowledge and beliefs. Fall 2026 I am teaching three intro courses and one 300 level philosophy of mind course and I will be teaching intro and a 300 level course in personal identity in Spring 2027. For my intro courses in Fall 2026, we set up a Minecraft server and are running embodied llm agents in the server to perform various experiments uses Dave Chalmers' book Reality+.
