Anatomical Explanations

* characteristic abnormalities in the cerebellum
	* However, Cerebellar damage inflicted by a stroke in a child usually produces tremors, swaying gait and abnormal eye movements--symptoms rarely seen in autism
	* and one does not see any of the symptoms typical of autism in patients with cerebellar disease.

Psychological Explanations

* a deficit in the ability to construct a "theory of other minds.
	* specialized neural circuitry in the brain allows us to create sophisticated hypotheses about the inner workings of other people's minds. 
	* These hypotheses, in turn, enable us to make useful predictions about others' behavior.
		* However, does not provide a complete explanation for the constellation of Page 2 of 5  seemingly unrelated symptoms of autism
